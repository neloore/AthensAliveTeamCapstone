Our visual identity

Visual Design Guide

Many Events. One Athens.

A shared visual language for Athens Alive: local landmarks, warm backgrounds, and lively color accents that bring our events together.

01 / Palette

Color with a purpose.

Beige creates a warm foundation. Blue organizes the hierarchy, orange highlights actions, and green adds energy through lines and supporting accents. The color specifications below follow our approved brand palette.

Lines and subtext accents.

Keep text readable

Use dark ink (#2b2b2b) for paragraphs on beige. Keep orange and green primarily in shapes, icons, buttons, and decorative accents; use dark text for small labels. A green underline can support a subheading while its words remain dark. Use dark labels on orange buttons and a visible keyboard focus outline.

03 / Typography

Merriweather Sans.

One type family keeps the identity consistent across web pages and supporting materials. Semibold headings create a clear hierarchy; light body text provides a softer contrast to the bold logo.
