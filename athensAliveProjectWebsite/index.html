Project Details

User Research

We conducted interviews, surveys, secondary research through social media, and client meetings to gain a better understanding of our consumers.

Branding Creation

Through our client feedback, we reinvented the branding elements for our client - including the collective's name, font, logo, and color palette.

Sponsor Packages

For our clients, we honed in on the branding creation to restructure sponsorship packages and corresponding decks and infographics to present.

Website Redesign

Utilizing our research, branding, and sponsorship material, we combined our efforts into a fully redesigned website for the collective festivals to use moving forward.
